# Zomato Restaurant Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Zomato restaurant data using Python to discover business insights about restaurant performance, pricing, customer ratings, cuisines, and city-wise trends.

The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), visualizations, and business recommendations.

---

## Objectives

- Clean and preprocess the dataset
- Explore restaurant performance
- Analyze customer ratings
- Compare pricing across restaurants
- Study cuisine popularity
- Generate business insights
- Recommend strategies for business growth

---

## Business Questions Answered

1. Which cities have the highest number of restaurants?
2. Which restaurants receive the highest customer ratings?
3. Which cuisines are most popular?
4. Which cities have the highest dining votes?
5. Which restaurants are marked as Best Sellers?
6. Does restaurant pricing affect customer ratings?
7. Which cities have the highest delivery ratings?
8. Does cuisine influence restaurant sales?
9. Do expensive restaurants receive better ratings?
10. Which cities have the highest customer demand?

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains information such as:

- Restaurant Name
- City
- Cuisine
- Dining Rating
- Delivery Rating
- Dining Votes
- Delivery Votes
- Price
- Best Seller Status

---

## Key Insights

- Most restaurants are concentrated in a few major cities.
- High-priced restaurants generally maintain good ratings.
- Popular cuisines dominate customer demand.
- Customer votes strongly indicate restaurant popularity.
- Best Seller restaurants receive higher engagement.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Expand successful restaurants into high-demand cities.
- Improve delivery quality for lower-rated restaurants.
- Promote popular cuisines through marketing campaigns.
- Monitor pricing strategies to remain competitive.
- Focus on customer satisfaction to increase ratings.
